# Maintained-fork notice

This repository is an ALENOC-maintained fork of Electrum-RVN-SIG's Ravencoin
adaptation of Electrum. It contains the original Electrum wallet, Ravencoin
conversion and asset work from the community, and later maintained changes. It
is not an official Ravencoin or Electrum release.

The project remains distributed under the MIT license in `LICENCE`. Historical
copyright, permission and third-party notices are preserved. This notice
identifies maintenance lineage; it does not claim authorship or ownership of
the original Electrum or Ravencoin work and adds no licensing restrictions.
